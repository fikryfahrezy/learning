#### Question 1: Estimating missed appointments from attendance data

Which of these challenges would predictive AI best help solve?

- A quality-control system identifies defects in products using photos uploaded from the factory floor.
- A dispatcher automatically assigns drivers and routes to minimize travel time across the city.
- A marketing team generates fresh blog posts and ad copy tailored to a seasonal campaign.
- A healthcare clinic estimates which patients are likely to miss appointments next week using past attendance data.

**Answer: A healthcare clinic estimates which patients are likely to miss appointments next week using past attendance data.**

Predictive AI uses patterns in historical data to estimate what will happen next, such as the likelihood of no-shows, so that teams can act proactively.

#### Question 2: A stop sign misread after prankster stickers

An autonomous vehicle misread a stop sign after a prankster placed stickers on it, causing the system to misinterpret the sign.

Which AI ethics implication for computer vision AI does this use case represent?

- Explainability
- Transparency
- Fairness
- Robustness

**Answer: Robustness**

In an adversarial attack, even small changes to visual inputs can lead to significant errors in AI interpretation.

#### Question 3: Sorting canned vegetables by type

A food bank conveyor belt scans and sorts canned vegetables by type for storage or delivery.

What computer vision AI skill does this represent?

- Recognizing shapes
- Classifying objects
- Generating new images

**Answer: Classifying objects**

It assigns the scan to a category such as peas, carrots, or potatoes based on distinctive features.

#### Question 4: Forecasting shipping delays from carrier and weather data

Which of these challenges would predictive AI best help solve?

- A call center dynamically schedules agents and sets queue priorities to meet service targets at the lowest cost.
- An e-commerce retailer forecasts shipping delays for upcoming orders based on carrier performance and weather data.
- A design tool creates new product mockups and variations from a text prompt.
- A smartphone app identifies plant species from photos taken by users.

**Answer: An e-commerce retailer forecasts shipping delays for upcoming orders based on carrier performance and weather data.**

Predictive AI estimates future outcomes, such as the probability of delivery delays, by learning from historical signals and current conditions.

#### Question 5: Analyzing the best flight routes

An airline wants to analyze the best flight routes by examining weather conditions, fuel consumption, and air traffic data.

Would decision-making AI or predictive AI be better suited for this task?

- Predictive AI
- Decision-making AI

**Answer: Decision-making AI**

Decision-making AI helps evaluate multiple options and select the one that makes the most sense based on real-time data.

#### Question 6: Setting a word count and tone for a resume summary

You’ve created the following prompt to update your resume: “Draft a LinkedIn summary for my marketing professional resume, which contains no more than 150 words and uses a friendly tone.”

Which component of the prompt helps facilitate a specific, controlled response from the AI tool?

- Role
- Context
- Output
- Constraints
- Task

**Answer: Constraints**

Directing the AI tool to create a resume summary with a word count limitation and a friendly tone as constraints defines your needs and directs the tool toward a more refined and usable result.

#### Question 7: Narrowing down wedding options within a budget

A wedding planner wants to narrow down the best options for cakes, venues, decorations, and music, based on client preferences and a set budget.

Which form of AI is best suited for this task?

- Computer vision AI
- Generative AI
- Decision-making AI
- Predictive AI

**Answer: Decision-making AI**

Decision-making AI is well-suited for comparing multiple options in a given context, enabling you to make more informed decisions.

#### Question 8: Suggesting titles at checkout based on browsing history

Which role of predictive AI in the AI ecosystem does this represent?

Offering insights and making recommendations helps surface relevant items that a customer is likely to enjoy.

#### Question 10: Brainstorming names for a new service

#### Question 11: A facial recognition system biased by cultural context

A city’s facial recognition system identifies potentially suspicious behavior in the visitor center. Trained on one cultural context, the system incorrectly classifies behaviors from other cultures, such as avoiding eye contact, which can lead to biased monitoring and evaluation.

Which AI ethics implication for computer vision AI does this use case represent?

- Robustness
- Fairness
- Explainability
- Transparency

**Answer: Fairness**

When training data lacks cultural diversity, it can cause biased outcomes, raising concerns about equal treatment in image recognition.

#### Question 12: Optimizing food delivery routes

#### Question 14: Assigning an expert role to improve usability suggestions
