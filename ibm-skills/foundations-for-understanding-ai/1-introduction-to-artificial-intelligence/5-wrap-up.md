### Question 1: Classifying spreadsheet records

A spreadsheet lists customer names, purchase totals, and order dates.

Which type of data does this describe?

- Metadata
- Semi-structured data
- Structured data
- Unstructured data

**Answer: Structured data**

Rows and columns make this classic structured data.

### Question 2: Classifying raw call recordings

A file folder contains thousands of raw customer service call recordings without labels.

Which type of data does this describe?

- Semi-structured data
- Unstructured data
- Structured data
- Metadata

**Answer: Unstructured data**

Audio files without a predefined format are unstructured.

### Question 3: Choosing the fastest truck route

A logistics platform reviews traffic patterns, fuel costs, and delivery deadlines to choose the fastest truck route every hour.

Which form of AI does this represent?

- Generative AI
- Predictive AI
- Computer vision AI
- Decision-making AI

**Answer: Decision-making AI**

Decision-making AI selects the optimal option after evaluating multiple factors.

### Question 4: Spotting cracked smartphone screens

Cameras on a factory manufacturing line can identify cracked smartphone screens before the phones are packaged for delivery.

Which form of AI does this represent?

- Generative AI
- Predictive AI
- Decision-making AI
- Computer vision AI

**Answer: Computer vision AI**

Computer vision AI interprets images to detect defects in real time.

### Question 5: Forecasting next month's demand

A retailer analyzes three years of sales history to help forecast next month's product demand.

Which form of AI does this represent?

- Generative AI
- Predictive AI
- Computer vision AI
- Decision-making AI

**Answer: Predictive AI**

Predictive AI uses historical patterns to estimate future outcomes.

### Question 6: Tallying daily sales in 1890

A shopkeeper in 1890 cranks a machine that tallies daily sales and prints totals on paper.

Which era of data processing does this represent?

- Era of AI
- Era of Programming
- Era of Tabulation

**Answer: Era of Tabulation**

Early tabulating machines organized and totaled structured data.

### Question 7: Scanning millions of social media posts

A research team needs to quickly scan millions of social media posts to gauge public sentiment.

Which AI strength does this represent?

- Learns continuously from new data
- Detects patterns
- Analyzes massive amounts of data

**Answer: Analyzes massive amounts of data**

AI can process large datasets much faster than humans.

Which form of AI does this represent?

- Computer vision AI

Which era of data processing does this represent?

- Era of AI
