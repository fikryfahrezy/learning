# Module Overview

## About this module

The real question is not, “Can machines think?” but “How can humans think differently when they work with machines?” Every day, you rely on technology that routes traffic, scans business reports, and even finishes your sentences. The “intelligence” that makes this possible is not magic; it’s the result of algorithms that learn from data at a scale no human can match. Understanding how AI systems think enables you to evaluate their recommendations, identify shortcomings, and imagine new ways to work with them.

Welcome to The Intelligence Behind AI module! In this module, you’ll explore the building blocks of artificial intelligence, compare human and machine learning, and discover AI capabilities, such as speed of computation, real-time adjustment, and scalability.

## Learning objectives

After completing this module, you should be able to:

- Differentiate between human learning and machine learning
- Identify features of machines that support solving difficult problems
- Identify applications for AI in the classroom and workplace
