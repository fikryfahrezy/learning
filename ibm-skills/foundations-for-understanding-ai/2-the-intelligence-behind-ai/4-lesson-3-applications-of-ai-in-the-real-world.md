## How people are using AI to solve problems

Generative AI transforms the way people approach challenges and create new solutions across various industries. As AI evolves, its role as a creative and practical tool will be increasingly important in daily life and work.

*Select each tab to learn more about how AI impacts your life.*

### Home and Daily Life

AI technologies, such as generative AI, are transforming how people manage their homes and routines. Smart home devices and virtual assistants automate tasks, improve energy efficiency, and enhance security. They also respond to voice commands, learn user preferences, and adapt to changing schedules. People also use generative AI to plan meals, build travel itineraries, or plan home improvement projects.

### Health and Wellness

AI and generative AI are being used to support proactive health management and everyday wellness. Applications analyze data from wearable devices, electronic health records, and medical images to detect early signs of illness and offer personalized guidance. Generative AI can help people create custom meal plans, exercise routines, or mental health journal prompts. These tools also support remote monitoring, making preventive care more accessible and continuous.

### Learning and Connection

AI and generative AI platforms support learning and connection by offering adaptive tutoring, real-time translation, and personalized educational content. Intelligent chatbots answer questions, while recommendation systems guide learners to relevant materials. Generative AI can also help users draft essays, practice conversations, or brainstorm ideas, making learning more interactive and creative. These tools can help people build knowledge, connect with others, and access support from anywhere.

---

AI-driven technologies are deeply integrated into many aspects of daily life, from managing homes and health to supporting learning and building stronger communities. Understanding these applications can help you recognize the value of AI in improving convenience, well-being, and accessibility.

## AI in the classroom

AI is also a powerful tool in classrooms and learning environments. AI tools make learning more engaging and tailored to individual needs.

*Select each section to learn how generative AI enhances and streamlines educational practices.*

### Personalized learning

**Personalized learning** pathways use generative AI to tailor lessons, activities, and resources to students’ strengths and needs. AI can analyze students’ performance and adjust the pace and content to help everyone succeed. This approach enables students to remain engaged and receive the support they need.

### Automated grading

**Automated grading** and feedback systems use AI to review assignments, quizzes, and written work quickly and accurately. This technology saves teachers time and gives students immediate feedback on their progress. Feedback helps students learn from their mistakes and improve more quickly.

### Educational Chatbots

AI-powered educational chatbots are virtual assistants that answer questions, explain concepts, and guide students through lessons in real time. These chatbots are available anytime, making it easier for students to get help when they need it.

## Workplace applications for AI

AI is changing the way people work across many industries. AI technologies save time and help businesses respond more quickly and accurately to challenges. As generative AI becomes more prevalent, workplaces continue to explore new ways to use it for enhanced efficiency and informed decision making.

*Select each card to explore how AI transforms the workplace.*

### Customer Experience and Engagement

AI helps businesses understand customers by analyzing data from habits, website visits, and feedback. This analysis enables personalized recommendations, instant chatbot replies, and anticipation of customer needs. For example, suggesting products based on past purchases to make shopping faster and more enjoyable.

### Risk Management and Security

AI protects businesses by monitoring for unusual activity, such as fraud or cyberattacks, in real time. It can identify patterns that humans might miss, automatically alert security teams, or block threats. Risk teams use generative AI to draft incident reports, create risk management plans, and summarize emerging threats.

### Operations and Logistics

AI helps companies streamline the movement and delivery of goods by forecasting demand, planning delivery routes, and scheduling equipment maintenance. This allows companies to save time and money while ensuring products reach customers quickly.

### Workforce and Talent Management

AI helps businesses find the best job candidates, monitor employee satisfaction, and suggest training opportunities. AI analyzes applications and workplace data, recommends individuals who are best suited for a job, and offers strategies to keep employees motivated.

AI enhances business by personalizing experiences, protecting assets, streamlining operations, and informing decisions. These real-world applications demonstrate how AI influences the way organizations operate and compete. Next, you’ll explore different applications of these AI tools in different industries.
