##Important Info About Travis and Account Registration

***Note - If you run out of credits, are unable to register for a Travis account, or, simply do not wish to use Travis**, then, please refer to the very next lecture which has guidance for GitHub Actions:*

*https://www.udemy.com/course/docker-and-kubernetes-the-complete-guide/learn/lecture/45192289#questions*

In the upcoming lecture, we will be signing up for an account with Travis. Since travis-ci.org is no longer operational, you will be redirected to sign up at:

https://www.travis-ci.com/

Along with this change, they have also updated their terms regarding free accounts and credits due to crypto mining abuse.

As a result, you will need to select a plan after registering:

Select Monthly Plans and then the free Trial Plan. This will give you **10,000 free credits** to use within 30 days.

**IMPORTANT - as part of the registration process, Travis now requires adding a Credit Card.**

*A free ‘Trial’ Plan, which any new user can select upon sign up, is a usage-based plan with an unlimited amount of users, which comes with a 10k trial pool of credits to start building right away. **Once these credits run out, they are not replenished, and users must subscribe to a higher plan to continue building**. Request OSS Credits allowance, or please consider one of our available plans. **Trial credits are valid only for 30 days**.*

https://docs.travis-ci.com/user/billing-overview/
