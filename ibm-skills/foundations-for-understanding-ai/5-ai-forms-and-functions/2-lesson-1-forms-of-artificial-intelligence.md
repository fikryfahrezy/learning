# Lesson 1: Forms of Artificial Intelligence

## What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. You encounter AI every day in voice assistants, recommendation systems, and fraud detection. AI learns from data, identifies trends, and even creates new content. The capabilities of AI often fall into three areas:

- **Language-based**, for example, reading, writing, translating, and answering questions
- **Perception-based**, for example, analyzing images, audio, and other sensory inputs
- **Decision making**, for example, weighing options and recommending actions

Examples of AI capabilities include a multilingual chatbot answering customer questions, a phone unlocking via facial recognition, or an online store suggesting products you might want.

> **Artificial intelligence**, or AI, is the ability of machines to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, making decisions, or understanding language.

Different forms of AI apply these capabilities in distinct ways:  

- **Predictive AI** uses historical data to forecast outcomes.
- **Decision-making AI** evaluates multiple factors to recommend the best course of action.
- **Computer vision AI** interprets visual information such as photos and videos.
- **Generative AI** creates new text, images, or music from patterns it learned.

AI typically follows these two steps:  

- **Analysis**, examining large datasets to find patterns
- **Prediction**, using those patterns to generate the most likely next words, images, or recommendations

Together, these approaches of analysis and prediction help people work faster, make informed decisions, and enhance experiences across industries.

## The primary forms of AI

AI tools impact many aspects of your daily and professional life. There are four primary forms of AI to help you perform specialized tasks, analyze data, make informed decisions, interpret existing visual content, and generate new content.

*Select each section to learn more about the different forms of AI and their uses.*

### Predictive AI

Predictive AI analyzes past data to forecast future events. This form of AI identifies patterns and trends to help people prepare and plan.

**Predictive AI excels at:**

- Forecasting sales, demand, or customer behavior
- Spotting early warning signs of problems, such as equipment failure
- Helping teams plan inventory, staffing, or finances

For example, a retail company uses predictive AI to estimate which products will sell well next season and automatically orders inventory in advance. Predictive AI helps reduce uncertainty and improve planning by providing people with information about a problem before it occurs.

### Decision-making AI

Decision-making AI helps users select the optimal option in situations with multiple variables. This form of AI compares data, weighs outcomes, and recommends or automates actions.

**Decision-making AI excels at:**

- Recommending actions based on rules and data
- Helping organizations respond quickly to complex problems
- Reducing human error in routine or high-pressure situations

For example, a shipping company uses decision-making AI to determine the fastest delivery route, taking into account factors such as traffic, weather, and delivery deadlines. Decision-making AI supports better choices, quicker responses, and more efficient use of time and resources.

### Computer vision AI

Computer vision AI allows machines to interpret and analyze images or video. This form of AI identifies objects, people, and movements.

**Computer vision AI excels at:**

- Scanning and sorting packages
- Recognizing faces for security systems
- Helping doctors read X-rays or medical scans
- Checking product quality on assembly lines

For example, a logistics company uses computer vision AI to scan and verify packages during shipping, so that nothing is missing or mislabeled. Using computer vision to automate tasks that require visual attention saves time and increases accuracy.

### Generative AI

Generative AI creates new content by learning from patterns in existing data. This form of AI generates text, images, music, or code based on previous experience or training.

**Generative AI excels at:**

- Drafting emails, reports, or social media posts
- Creating code snippets or technical documentation
- Generating images or design ideas for marketing or product concepts
- Brainstorming or filling in missing information

For example, a marketing team uses generative AI to create social media posts and design ad graphics tailored to various target audiences. Generative AI enables teams to work more efficiently and creatively when producing content.
