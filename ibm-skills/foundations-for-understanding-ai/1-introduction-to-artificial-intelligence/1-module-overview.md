# Module Overview

## About this module

Alan Turing, the pioneer of modern AI, said, “We can only see a short distance ahead, but we can see plenty there that needs to be done.”

Did you know that AI can already caption your photos, offer insights on the weather, and translate a conversation while you speak? Once you understand the basics, those same tools can help you work faster, identify patterns, and make better-informed decisions every day.

Welcome to the Introduction to Artificial Intelligence module! In this module, you’ll explore what AI is, why it matters in a data-driven world, and how its core capabilities can help solve real-world problems.
